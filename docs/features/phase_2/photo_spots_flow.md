# Photo Spots Admin and Showcase

## Purpose

Ops staff curate the public and in-app spot catalogue.

## Main Flow

1. Staff reviews pending spots.
2. Staff approves, rejects, or edits spot metadata.
3. Public showcase shows approved spots only.

## Success State

Spot quality is controlled by staff while public visitors see safe approved
content.
